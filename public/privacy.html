Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Vows.ng holds two kinds of information: details about the couple who created a wedding page, and details about the guests they invite. Guests never gave us their information directly, which is why we treat it with particular care and never use it for anything except the wedding it belongs to.

1. Who we are

Vows.ng ("we", "us") provides free wedding websites for couples in Nigeria. For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA") we act as a data controller for account information, and as a data processor for the guest lists that couples upload — those remain the couple's data, held on their instructions.

You can reach us at Hit4hiit@gmail.com for any question about this policy or about information we hold.

2. What we collect

From couples

From guests directly

If a guest records a gift, we store the name they give, the amount they say they transferred, and their invitation code if they enter one. We never see, hold or process the money itself. Gifts are transferred by the guest, from their own bank, straight to the couple's account. We are not a payment service and we hold no funds at any point.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for or store card details, bank passwords, PINs or BVNs.
  • We do not track guests across other websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with advertisers.
  • We do not use guest lists to market anything to those guests.

4. Our lawful basis

Under section 25 of the NDPA we rely on:

  • Contract — we process a couple's account details because we cannot provide the service they asked for without them.
  • Legitimate interest — a couple may add their guests' details in order to invite them, in the same way they would address an envelope. Guests may ask the couple, or us, to remove their details at any time.
  • Consent — a guest chooses to submit an RSVP or record a gift.

7. How long we keep it

  • Wedding pages and guest lists are kept while the account is active.
  • If you delete your wedding, its guest list and codes are deleted with it.
  • An account inactive for 24 months after the wedding date may be deleted, after we email you first.
  • You can ask us to delete everything at any time, and we will do so within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive a copy in a portable format. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you believe we have handled your information wrongly.

To exercise any of these, email [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS]. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are a guest and would rather not appear on a wedding page, contact us and we will remove your details, and tell the couple that you asked.

9. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit.
  • Access is enforced by server-side database rules, not by hiding things in the page.
  • Invitation codes are randomly generated and cannot be listed or guessed in bulk.
  • Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and never stored by us.

No system is perfect. If a breach occurs that is likely to risk your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and tell affected people directly.

10. Children

Vows.ng is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. A guest list may include a child's name where a couple invites a family; that is the couple's decision as the family's own record, and we use it for nothing else.

11. Cookies

We use only what is necessary to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and there is nothing here to opt out of.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will email account holders before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
